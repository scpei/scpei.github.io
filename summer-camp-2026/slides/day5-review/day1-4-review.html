Pixels and channels
11 / 30

An image is a grid of numerical values.

Pixel position stores spatial structure; color channels store intensity. Resolution changes how much detail can be represented and processed.

Width × height × channels

Pixels are small measurements.

A computer receives arrays of values rather than a ready-made understanding of “frog,” “leaf,” or “water.”

Meaning emerges from coordinated patterns across many values.

Diffusion
13 / 30

A diffusion model learns to remove noise step by step.

Training creates noisy examples at many levels. Generation starts with noise and repeatedly predicts a cleaner state.

Many modest corrections

Structure emerges gradually.

Large composition often settles before fine detail. One prompt can still produce several valid denoising trajectories.

Forward: add noise · Reverse: predict cleaner states

Audio representation
25 / 30

Audio models choose what kind of sequence to predict.

Waveforms store signal over time; spectrograms show frequency over time; tokens and latents offer more compact representations.

The representation defines the task

One sound, several possible encodings.

Fine waveforms preserve detail. Spectrograms reveal frequency structure. Tokens can make long sequences easier to model.

Sound is data changing through time.

Video representation
27 / 30

Video adds a time dimension to image generation.

Each frame must work as an image, while the sequence must preserve motion, identity, background, and cause-and-effect.

Space + time

A video is a connected stack of frames.

Position and pose advance over time. Compression makes generation practical, but the model still has to coordinate the whole sequence.

A beautiful frame does not guarantee a believable video.

Temporal coherence
28 / 30

The sequence must agree with itself.

Temporal coherence means identity, object structure, lighting, motion, and setting remain connected across time.

Inspect the whole sequence

Errors can hide between frames.

Watch for identity drift, flicker, teleporting motion, changing backgrounds, and objects that quietly reshape.

Frames share information so the story remains continuous.
