Teaching + Lab Schedule · Days 1–403 / 09
Day 1 · Generative AI & LLMs
04 / 09

Language models build text one token at a time.

Start with familiar AI, look inside the prediction engine, and finish with the judgment needed to use it responsibly.

What we will learn
1

Foundations

AI, machine learning, generative models, and tokens.

2

Mechanisms

Next-token prediction, probability, Transformers, and attention.

3

Judgment

Training data, limitations, verification, and responsible use.

LAB OUTPUTExplore and explain a small language model

Day 2 · Image Generation
05 / 09

Images emerge through guided denoising.

Connect pixels and latent representations to diffusion, prompting, coherence, and human review.

What we will learn
1

Represent

Pixels, color channels, resolution, features, and latent space.

2

Generate

Noise, diffusion, iterative denoising, and text conditioning.

3

Direct

Prompt specificity, composition, style, coherence, and disclosure.

LAB OUTPUTDesign, generate, inspect, and revise an image
Summer Camp · AI Foundations05 / 09
Day 3 · Style Transformation
06 / 09

Keep the meaning. Change the voice.

Learn how style works, guide transformations across text and images, and defend creative choices.

What we will learn
1

Recognize

Tone, rhythm, palette, texture, composition, and audience.

2

Transform

Conditional generation, text rewriting, image-to-image, and style transfer.

3

Revise

Protect meaning and identity while checking drift, bias, and attribution.

LAB OUTPUTCreate two voices and two visual styles

Day 4 · Generative Audio & Video
07 / 09

Sound and motion become a complete story.

Move from signals and frames to continuity, directing, production, presentation, and trust.

What we will learn
1

Generate sound

Waveforms, speech, prosody, music, effects, and audio layers.

2

Generate motion

Frames, video diffusion, temporal coherence, camera, and action.

3

Tell responsibly

Storyboard, assemble, review, disclose, and recognize deepfakes.

LAB OUTPUTProduce and present a short multimodal scene

Day 5 · Project Capstone
08 / 09

Day 5 is your capstone project studio.

Start with a one-hour tutorial on the three project tools, then plan, build, test, revise, and present a project that connects Days 1–4.

What we will do
1

Tool tutorial · 1 hour

Meet the three project tools and learn the core workflow for each one.

2

Plan, build & test

Define the goal and audience, create the pieces, and test how they work together.

3

Revise & present

Improve the weakest part, document decisions, and share the final project.

PROJECT OUTPUTA complete capstone project + presentation
