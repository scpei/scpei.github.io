01 / Why this camp

From curious user
to thoughtful creator.

Generative AI can feel like magic. This course opens the box. Students build small, visible experiments that reveal how models work—then practice the judgment that good creative work requires.

  • Explain core ideas in plain language
  • Experiment without an API key or paid software
  • Spot drift, errors, and misleading outputs
  • Use AI responsibly and disclose its role

02 / The five-day journey

One new medium
every day.

Each day moves from a clear concept to a guided experiment, a creative challenge, and a short reflection. The week builds toward an original presentation.

01

Language

Inside a Tiny
Language Generator

Tokenize text, build a transparent next-word model, change its temperature, and learn why fluent answers still need verification.

  • Tokens & probability
  • Training data
  • NotebookLM
02

Images

From Pixels & Noise
to Prompted Images

Inspect RGB pixels, add and remove noise, trace a diffusion pipeline, and design precise prompts while auditing visual coherence.

  • RGB arrays
  • Diffusion
  • Prompt design
03

Style

Same Meaning,
New Voice

Separate content from style, transform text and images, then check whether facts, identity, or meaning drifted along the way.

  • Style fingerprints
  • Transfer models
  • Meaning audit
04

Time-based media

Sound, Motion
& Story

See sound as samples and video as frames. Generate speech, create in-between motion, and inspect a sequence for continuity.

  • Speech synthesis
  • Frame interpolation
  • Consent
05

Capstone

Multimodal
Knowledge Director

Choose a question, research it, decide which medium helps, and direct a clear ten-slide story for a live audience.

  • Research
  • Creative direction
  • Presentation

03 / The student experience

Less watching.
More making.

No previous coding experience is required. Prepared notebooks let beginners change one meaningful variable at a time, while optional challenges give experienced students room to go deeper.

01

See it

Begin with an observable phenomenon, not a wall of terminology.

02

Change it

Run an experiment and adjust a visible input, setting, or creative choice.

03

Question it

Compare results, identify failure modes, and look for supporting evidence.

04

Direct it

Revise with purpose and explain the choices behind the final work.

“The goal is not just to make an output. It is to understand, inspect, and improve it.”

Core Python activities run in Google Colab. Most work needs only a current browser and a Google account; selected model demonstrations may be preloaded by the instructor.

04 / What students take away

Creative confidence,
with evidence.

A

A working mental model

Students can explain what tokens, probability, pixels, noise, samples, and frames contribute to generative systems.

B

A critical eye

Students learn to detect hallucinations, coherence problems, identity drift, unsupported claims, and missing disclosures.

C

A creator’s workflow

Students practice moving from question to research, generation, audit, revision, and thoughtful presentation.
